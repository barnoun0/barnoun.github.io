📓 what is docs.log?

This is my personal documentation hub — a raw, unfiltered archive of everything I learn about systems, networking, Linux, and software development. No SEO fluff, no ads, just notes that I wish I had when I started.

Everything here is written for future me, and maybe for you too.

🧑‍💻 who am i?

I'm a systems engineer who loves breaking things to understand how they work. Over the years, I've collected hundreds of markdown notes, and I finally decided to put them online.

When I'm not debugging kernel panics or chasing packet drops, I write about infrastructure, automation, and the occasional coding rabbit hole.

Linux Kubernetes C/C++ Go TCP/IP

📡 why github pages?

Because it's free, it's built on Git (version control for writing), and it keeps me honest — once a post is published, I own it. No platform lock-in, just static files that will work forever.

This entire blog is plain HTML/CSS/JS. No build steps, no databases — just content.

⚡ the short version

I'm barnoun. I'm a teenager who breathes tech. If it has wires, a kernel, or a packet header — I'm into it. I break things on purpose so I can learn how to fix them. Sometimes I break things on accident. That's also learning.

🕸️ what i'm obsessed with

Networking — VLANs, BGP, routing, the dark art of why packets disappear. I love knowing exactly how data moves across the world.

Robotics — embedded systems, sensors, actuators, making stupid things move intelligently. ROS? Played with it. Soldering iron? Burned myself (worth it).

Linux — I distro-hop like it's a sport. Arch? Been there. NixOS? Currently breaking. I live in the terminal. The GUI is just a suggestion.

Linux TCP/IP ROS C++ Python Bash

🔨 create & destroy

I build homelabs in my closet. I set up Pihole, break it, fix it, then add something stupid like a self-hosted Minecraft server just because I can. I write scripts that automate things that probably don't need automation — but it's fun.

If I'm not at a terminal, I'm probably taking apart an old router or trying to make a robot do something useless but impressive.

📖 why this blog exists

This is my digital brain dump. Every time I figure something out — a networking trick, a Linux quirk, a robotics hack — I write it down here. For future me. For you. For anyone who's also a curious teenager staying up too late trying to understand how things really work.

No ads. No SEO garbage. Just raw notes from a tech nerd who loves this stuff.
